## Run the updated team

1. Save your changes. Complete any separate save action shown on the screen.
2. Select **Run** to run the team again.
3. In **Logs**, check the new run's time and open **Plan Reviewer → content**.

## Review the result

Use these questions to review the new itinerary.

- Does it include activities that match your chosen interests?
- Does it contain no more than two sightseeing activities and at least one rest break?
- Does it avoid adding places or unsupported times and prices beyond the supplied information?
- Does it explain the revisions or state that the draft already met the rule?

If the previous itinerary already met the conditions, the changes may be small. Focus on **whether the requested conditions are followed**, rather than how different the answer looks.

Rerunning the whole team can also change earlier agents' answers. Do not assume every difference was caused by the single instruction you edited. Mark any unmet conditions and review them with the facilitator.

## Optional practice

If time allows, change another interest or review condition and run the team again. For a different destination, replace the activity information and verify its accuracy separately.
