In this workshop, you will use a prepared AI team to draft a one-day Hong Kong itinerary. You will explore the three roles and how they pass information, then change the travel preferences and instructions to adapt the draft.

## What is an AI agent?

**An AI agent is an AI system that determines and carries out the tasks needed to achieve a given goal.**

In this workshop, we explore a simple structure with separate sightseeing, food and itinerary roles, where agents use earlier results to carry out their tasks.

## The three AI roles

| Role | Name on screen | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sightseeing specialist | Sightseeing Planner | Select sightseeing activities and identify their areas |
| Food specialist | Food Planner | Choose food examples matching the selected sightseeing areas and food preference |
| Itinerary organizer | Trip Planner | Combine both recommendations into an itinerary with a rest break |

## How do the roles work together?

The sightseeing specialist selects places and their areas. The food specialist then chooses food options that match those areas and the traveler's preferences. The itinerary organizer combines both recommendations into a day plan with a meal and a rest break.

The roles run in sequence because each later task needs the result of the earlier one.

## What you need

Prepare a laptop, an internet connection and access to your email for account verification. The exercise runs in a web browser and does not require programming experience.

Keep this guide in one tab and open the workshop services in other tabs. If you already have an account, sign in instead of creating another.

## Services used in the workshop

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Sim | View the team, edit its instructions and run it |
| OpenRouter | Connect to AI models to send requests and receive answers |

An **API key** is a private authentication code that allows Sim to use AI models through your OpenRouter account. It is different from your login password but should be protected in the same way.

The exercise is configured to request a free model. Usage or access restrictions may affect availability. If you see a payment or upgrade request, contact the facilitator before making a purchase.
