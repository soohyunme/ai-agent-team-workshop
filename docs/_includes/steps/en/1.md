After watching the facilitator's travel-team demonstration, you will create and connect three agents in a new workflow. You will divide the work among specialists, pass the information they need and check whether the team's result meets your travel goal.

## What you will learn

The goal is to learn **how to divide work among AI agents and connect their results to complete a shared task**. The Hong Kong itinerary is an example for practicing this process.

The aim is for you to apply this approach to dividing roles and coordinating their work after the workshop, building and using multi-agent teams that meet your needs in learning, work and everyday life.

## What is an AI agent?

**An AI agent is an AI system that determines and carries out the tasks needed to achieve a given goal.**

In this workshop, we explore a simple structure with separate sightseeing, food and itinerary roles, where agents use earlier results to carry out their tasks.

## What is a multi-agent system?

A multi-agent system has multiple agents sharing information and dividing responsibilities to work toward a common goal. In this exercise, three agents pass results in an order defined by a person. We do not cover agents choosing their own roles or execution order.

## The three AI roles

| Role | Name on screen | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sightseeing specialist | Sightseeing Planner | Select sightseeing activities and identify their areas |
| Food specialist | Food Planner | Choose food examples matching the selected sightseeing areas and food preference |
| Itinerary organizer | Trip Planner | Combine both recommendations into an itinerary with a rest break |

## How do the roles work together?

The sightseeing specialist selects places and their areas. The food specialist then chooses food options that match those areas and the traveler's preferences. The itinerary organizer combines both recommendations into a day plan with a meal and a rest break.

Agents can work in sequence or at the same time, depending on the task. In this exercise, the food specialist uses the sightseeing specialist's results, and the itinerary organizer combines both results, so the team runs in sequence.

## Why divide the work?

We use three specialists to practice **dividing responsibilities and inspecting intermediate results**. A single AI can handle a simple travel request; more agents are not always better.

You will leave with **a travel team you built** and **a sketch of a team for your own task**. The aim is to explain each role's responsibility, what it passes to the next role and what would need to change for another task.

## Watch the completed team first

The facilitator will run the travel team once. Participants do not need to import or run the template now. Observe **the three roles' execution order, the information passed and where the final itinerary appears**.

You will then build the same structure together. Shared data and prompt examples are provided, but you will configure and connect the roles yourself. If studying independently, skip the demonstration and follow the steps in order.

## What you need

Prepare a laptop, an internet connection and access to your email for account verification. The exercise runs in a web browser and does not require programming experience.

Keep this guide in one tab and open the workshop services in other tabs.

## Services used in the workshop

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Sim](https://www.sim.ai/) | View the team, edit its instructions and run it |
| [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/) | Connect to AI models to send requests and receive answers |

Sim is our hands-on tool for arranging and running this structure, not the only way to build agents. Rather than focusing on Sim's features, focus on **which roles you need, what they should pass on and how to check their results**. These principles also apply when building agent teams with other tools.

In this exercise, Sim sends requests to AI models through OpenRouter. You will prepare the **API key** needed to connect the two services in the next step.
