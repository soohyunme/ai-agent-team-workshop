In this workshop, you will use a prepared AI team to draft a one-day Hong Kong itinerary. You will explore the three roles and how they pass information, then change the travel preferences and instructions to adapt the draft.

## What you need

Prepare a laptop, an internet connection and access to your email for account verification. The exercise runs in a web browser and does not require programming experience.

Keep this guide in one tab and open the workshop services in other tabs. If you already have an account, sign in instead of creating another.

## The three AI roles

In this exercise, an **agent** is an AI assigned a particular role and instructions. The agents divide the work and pass intermediate results to produce a single itinerary draft.

| Role | Name on screen | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sightseeing specialist | Sightseeing Planner | Select sightseeing activities and identify their areas |
| Food specialist | Food Planner | Choose food examples matching the selected sightseeing areas and food preference |
| Itinerary organizer | Trip Planner | Combine both recommendations into an itinerary with a rest break |

A **template** is a prepared example with the roles and connections already in place. In this template, the three agents pass answers in a fixed sequence.

The team uses supplied activity information. It does not search live information or make bookings. Before using the draft for travel, check opening hours, prices, travel times, accessibility and other practical details separately.

## Services used in the workshop

| Service | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Sim | View the team, edit its instructions and run it |
| OpenRouter | Connect to AI models to send requests and receive answers |

An **API key** is a private authentication code that allows Sim to use AI models through your OpenRouter account. It is different from your login password but should be protected in the same way.

The template is configured to request a free model. Usage or access restrictions may affect availability. If you see a payment or upgrade request, contact the facilitator before making a purchase.

## How do the roles work together?

The sightseeing specialist passes the **selected places and their areas** to the food specialist. The food specialist selects **food examples matching those areas and your food preference**. The itinerary organizer combines both results with a rest break.

The sequence matters because each later role needs the earlier result. Matching area labels do not establish actual travel distances or times.

Food options are **fictional workshop examples**, not actual restaurants or menus.
