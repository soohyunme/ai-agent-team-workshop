You will build a hotel review response system that reads guest feedback and prepares a response. Divide the work among three roles and pass the information they need to produce internal follow-up proposals and a guest reply draft.

## What you will learn

The goal is to learn **how to divide work among AI agents and connect their results to complete a shared task**. Hotel review response is our practice example.

The aim is for you to apply this approach to build and use multi-agent teams that meet your needs in learning, work and everyday life.

## What is an AI agent?

**An AI agent is an AI system that determines and carries out the tasks needed to achieve a given goal.**

Here, we divide the work into feedback analysis, operational proposals and guest communication.

## What is a multi-agent system?

A multi-agent system has multiple agents sharing information and responsibilities toward a common goal. This exercise uses an order defined by a person; the agents do not choose their own roles or execution order.

## The three AI roles

| Role | Name on screen | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feedback analyst | Review Analyst | Identify positive feedback and concerns with original evidence |
| Operations advisor | Operations Advisor | Propose internal checks and improvements for the concerns |
| Guest communicator | Guest Reply Writer | Use earlier results to draft a reply for the guest |

## How do the roles work together?

The analyst identifies feedback and evidence. The operations advisor uses that analysis to propose internal follow-up. The reply writer reads both results and the original review to draft a response.

Agents can also work concurrently when tasks allow. Here, later roles need earlier results, so they run in sequence. **An operational proposal is not a completed action.** Check that the next role does not turn it into a factual claim or a promise.

## Why divide the work?

One AI could write a simple review response. We use three roles to practice **separating analysis, internal proposals and external communication, and reviewing intermediate results**.

You will leave with **a hotel review response system you built** and **a sketch for your own task**. The goal is to explain who does what and what information they pass on.

## Watch the completed team first

The facilitator will run the hotel review response system once. Participants do not need to import or run the template now. Observe **the three roles' execution order, the information passed and where the reply draft appears**.

You will then build the same structure together. Shared data and prompt examples are provided, but you will configure and connect the roles yourself. If studying independently, skip the demonstration and follow the steps in order.

Keep this guide in one tab and open the workshop services in other tabs.
